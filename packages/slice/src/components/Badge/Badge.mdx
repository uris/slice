        Custom backgroundColor and textColor override the variant independently. Omitted colors retain the preset. Choose readable color combinations.

        ## Behavior

        Padding uses 2px vertically and 6px horizontally, combining the theme’s 4px and 2px spacing tokens for the sides.
        Numeric counts above 99 display as 99+. Strings are shown as labels without numeric conversion. Null, undefined, blank strings, NaN, and infinite numbers render nothing. Numeric zero is hidden by default.

        ## Accessibility
        Badge defaults to role="status", aria-live="polite", and aria-atomic="true". Supply an aria-label to explain the count, including the full number when the display is capped. Use hideZero={false} to keep zero mounted for later updates. Actions belong on a labeled control.
