# Chip

Display compact tags or actions with text and an optional icon. label takes
precedence over children; numeric children such as 0 are supported.

## Behavior

Providing onClick creates a native button with type=button by default. Without it,
the content is static. selected is controlled by your app. onRemove adds a separate
remove button; after its exit transition, your handler updates the list. The wrapper
animates layout when selected is supplied, using Water easing over 150ms.
Chip owns removal presence internally. Its remove button fades the chip to zero opacity and scales it to 0.9 over 150ms with Water easing, then calls `onRemove` once so the parent can update its list. No presence wrapper is needed. Reduced motion skips the transition.

Default outer padding compensates for space inside SVG icons: subtract spacing-xs
(4px) for m–xl text, or spacing-xxs (2px) for xs/s. A left icon adjusts the left edge;
a right icon or remove button adjusts the right edge. Icon-only chips adjust both
edges. This leaves the icon-to-text gap unchanged. Explicit uniform or side padding
overrides the adjustment; individual sides take priority over uniform padding.

## Accessibility

Action chips support Enter and Space, a visible focus indicator, and native
disabled behavior. Text supplies their name; give icon-only buttons an aria-label.
Icons are decorative and do not add tab stops. Selected action buttons expose
aria-pressed. Main and remove buttons are siblings, never nested. Static content
has no automatic button role or tab stop. Tooltips support focus and hover.
Water color, layout, and exit transitions respect reduced-motion preferences. Reduced motion removes chips immediately, including with custom transitions. Exiting chips are hidden from assistive technology and cannot receive input; the app should move focus to a remaining chip or another appropriate control.

## Events

onClick creates the main native button and handles its activation. onRemove adds
a separate removal action; the chip exits before notifying the parent to remove it. Both callbacks receive
native button mouse events, including keyboard-generated clicks. Disabled chips
emit neither callback.

onToolTip: `(tip: ToolTip | null) => void`. Receives tooltip text and the root ref
on hover or focus, with a mouse event for hover only. null dismisses the tooltip
after activation or when both hover and focus have left.

Other native events match the main button or static content. Mouse and focus handlers compose with
tooltip behavior. Prevent default on mouse enter or focus to suppress the request.
